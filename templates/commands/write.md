## Outline

You are executing the writing process to produce the final content piece, based on the detailed task breakdown in `/specs/[FEATURE_NUMBER]-[CONTENT_SLUG]/tasks.md`, and guided by the constitution, specification, and plan.

Follow this execution flow:

1.  **Load All Artifacts**:
    *   Load the writing constitution from `/memory/constitution.md`.
    *   Load the writing specification from `/specs/[FEATURE_NUMBER]-[CONTENT_SLUG]/spec.md`.
    *   Load the writing plan from `/specs/[FEATURE_NUMBER]-[CONTENT_SLUG]/plan.md`.
    *   Load the research findings (if available) from `/specs/[FEATURE_NUMBER]-[CONTENT_SLUG]/research.md`.
    *   Load the writing tasks from `/specs/[FEATURE_NUMBER]-[CONTENT_SLUG]/tasks.md`.

2.  **Execute Tasks Sequentially**: Go through each task in `/specs/[FEATURE_NUMBER]-[CONTENT_SLUG]/tasks.md` and generate the corresponding content.
    *   **Drafting**: For each writing task, generate the content for the specified section/paragraph.
    *   **Compliance Check**: Ensure strict adherence to the principles outlined in the constitution (e.g., tone, style, ethical guidelines).
    *   **Research Integration**: Appropriately integrate and cite information from the research findings.
    *   **Logical Flow**: Verify logical consistency and smooth transitions between sections, as per the writing plan.
    *   **Media Integration**: Integrate media elements (images, diagrams, code examples) as planned, ensuring correct placement and formatting.
    *   **Review/Refine**: After initial drafting, perform a self-review against completion criteria.

3.  **Final Polish and Proofreading**: Once all drafting tasks are complete, conduct a comprehensive review:
    *   **Overall Cohesion**: Ensure the entire content flows logically and achieves the specified purpose.
    *   **Grammar and Spelling**: Perform a final check for linguistic errors.
    *   **Formatting**: Verify consistent and correct Markdown/output formatting.

4.  **Output**: Write the completed content to `/specs/[FEATURE_NUMBER]-[CONTENT_SLUG]/[CONTENT_SLUG].md` or a similar appropriate output file based on the content type.
